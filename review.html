Second Pass: the entry in one page

A checker, a protocol, an evidence log and a ten-minute drill for the review that comes after an AI assistant drafts the memo.

The problem

In diligence rooms this summer I read management's explanations of the variances and tested them against the ledger, and the ones that read best were not always the ones that tied. In the home services company my family runs I have watched the close memo get signed at the end of a long day. An AI assistant drafts those explanations now, and they read better than what a person writes on deadline.

The case in the drill is built, not captured. Halyard is a made-up company, and its June memo reads the way an AI assistant writes close commentary, with the mistakes they make planted line by line so they can be taught and scored.

What was built

    Play the ten-minute drill

  3. The protocol

    It fixes the scope and the threshold, puts the four checks ahead of the two questions only a person answers, and names who may sign.

  4. The record

    Every run records the call, the basis behind it, the error type and the time under an organization code. The participant downloads that record as a file, and sending it anywhere is a separate voluntary step.

Governance

The objective is one sentence. No commentary drafted with AI assistance is released until a second pass ties every figure to the ledger and a named reviewer signs the two judgment questions.

Four checks are mechanical and come first. Recompute every figure, test every direction word against the sign, list every account clearing both legs of the threshold, and name the ones no sentence mentions. Two questions are left for a person to sign, and they are the ones a machine cannot answer: whether the driver named in the sentence is supported by a document on file, and whether that driver belongs to the period the sentence covers.

Two people may not sign the second pass. The preparer may not, and neither may anyone who prompted the assistant for this memo.

Scope
Does
Today it runs the four mechanical checks, holds the evidence log, trains the two judgment calls, and records the basis a player gives for every one of the nineteen calls.
Does not yet
It does not yet judge a driver or a timing claim, match an account loosely, or read Excel. Four questions the form still marks required are not asked, and the drill posts a placeholder value each question accepts rather than anything that should be read as an answer. Those four columns are dropped from the findings rather than averaged.
Next
Next is the Excel template, and the checks running on every AI memo before a reviewer reads it.

The product is second-pass-drill 1.3.0. The two cases are versioned outside the page, as halyard-v3 for the fourteen practice lines and brightwater-v3 for the five assessment lines, both dated 13 September 2026, and the data notice is notice-2026-09-13. All four stamps travel with every response and with every local record, and responses scored against different case versions are never pooled. Responses scored against the retired brightwater-v2 measured a different thing and are excluded.

Round one is practice with a reveal after every call. Round two is scored at the end: no reveal between lines, no running score, no streak and no progress track, only a count of five, with every verdict arriving together once the fifth call is in. Every figure in that memo ties and every direction word is right, so the five lines test causal evidence and nothing else.

A data notice is read before anything is collected, and it says plainly that a codename is a pseudonym and not anonymity. Completion never depends on sharing: the run ends with a local record the participant downloads and a separate voluntary send. A sent run is labelled sent but unconfirmed, because the receiver returns no receipt this page can read, and a retry reuses the same attempt identifier so it cannot double count.

Every sentence a card shows under On file is a verified case assumption. A document that is not listed was not supplied, which is not a claim that it does not exist, so an unsupported sentence is not established rather than false. A flag can be correct while the reason behind it is wrong, and the reveal says so.

How the case and its memo were made is written down in PROVENANCE.md, the case files and what changed in them are in cases/README.md, and a live drafting run against the same ledger is recorded separately in what a real AI draft got wrong.

Replicability

The checker reads an account, a prior balance and a current balance, which is what every general ledger exports, so the ledger it runs on can come from any system. The protocol governs the memo rather than the model that drafted it, so the tool can change without the control changing. The threshold is a setting rather than a rule of the page, and the drill runs inside a chapter meeting in ten minutes, which is what makes it something a small firm can adopt without a project.
