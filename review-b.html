01The problem

In diligence rooms this summer I read management's explanations of the variances and tested them against the ledger, and the ones that read best were not always the ones that tied. In the home services company my family runs I have watched the close memo get signed at the end of a long day. An AI assistant drafts those explanations now, and they read better than what a person writes on deadline.

The case in the drill is built rather than captured. Halyard is a made-up company, and its June memo reads the way an AI assistant writes close commentary, with the mistakes they make planted line by line so they can be taught and scored.

It gave a cause for all fourteen accounts and the ledger supports none of them. The machine does not get the arithmetic wrong, it gets the causes wrong, fluently. From the live drafting run against the same ledger, 13 September 2026, recorded in the evidence note.

That run is why the control sits where it does. A reviewer who spends the hour recomputing variances is checking the part that was already right, and the reasons are where the work is.

02Governance

The objective is one sentence. No commentary drafted with AI assistance is released until a second pass ties every figure to the ledger and a named reviewer signs the two judgment questions.

  1. 1Recompute

    Every figure in the memo is recomputed from the prior and the current balance.

  2. 2Direction

    Every direction word is tested against the sign of the movement it describes.

  3. 3Coverage

    Every account clearing both legs of the threshold is listed, and the ones no sentence mentions are named.

  4. 4Sign-off

    A named reviewer signs the two questions a machine cannot answer, whether a document on file supports the driver in the sentence and whether that driver belongs to the period.

Two people may not sign the second pass. The preparer may not, and neither may anyone who prompted the assistant for this memo.

04Scope

Does
Today it runs the four mechanical checks, holds the evidence log, trains the two judgment calls, and records the basis a player gives for every one of the nineteen calls.
Does not yet
It does not yet judge a driver or a timing claim, match an account loosely, or read Excel. Four questions the form still marks required are not asked, and the drill posts a placeholder value each question accepts rather than anything that should be read as an answer.
Next
Next is the Excel template, and the checks running on every AI memo before a reviewer reads it.

05Replicability

The checker reads an account, a prior balance and a current balance, which is what every general ledger exports, so the ledger it runs on can come from any system.

The protocol governs the memo rather than the model that drafted it, so the tool can change without the control changing.

The threshold is a setting rather than a rule of the page, and the drill runs inside a chapter meeting in ten minutes, which is what makes it something a small firm can adopt without standing up a project.

06Versions and record

The stamps in the margin travel with every response and with every local record. Responses scored against different case versions are never pooled, and responses scored against the retired brightwater-v2 measured a different thing and are excluded.

Round one is practice with a reveal after every call. Round two is scored at the end, with no reveal between lines, no running score, no streak and no progress track, only a count of five, and every verdict arrives together once the fifth call is in. Every figure in that memo ties and every direction word is right, so the five lines test causal evidence and nothing else.

A data notice is read before anything is collected, and it says plainly that a codename is a pseudonym and not anonymity. Completion never depends on sharing, because the run ends with a local record the participant downloads and a separate voluntary send. A sent run is labelled sent but unconfirmed, since the receiver returns no receipt this page can read, and a retry reuses the same attempt identifier so it cannot double count.

Every sentence a card shows under On file is a verified case assumption. A document that is not listed was not supplied, which is not a claim that it does not exist, so an unsupported sentence is not established rather than false, and a flag can be correct while the reason behind it is wrong.

How the case and its memo were made is written down in PROVENANCE.md, the case files and what changed in them are in cases/README.md, and the live drafting run against the same ledger is recorded in what a real AI draft got wrong.
