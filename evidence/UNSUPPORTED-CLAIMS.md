# Unsupported claims in the six drafts, by class

Every claim in the six drafts of 13 September 2026 that goes beyond the ledger the model was given,
sorted into four classes, with the exact text behind every count. The classification was made on
13 September 2026 after the third independent review, by one reader. A second reader has not yet
classified them, and the items most likely to move under a second reading are listed at the end.

The runs are not one setup. The two Halyard drafts are the retained development-session run,
written inside the session that was building the exercise. The Brightwater and Kestrel drafts are
fresh-context runs, written in contexts that held only the ledger. [EVIDENCE-NOTE.md](EVIDENCE-NOTE.md)
explains why, and why that makes Halyard against the other two a comparison of setups as much as
of ledgers.

## The four classes

| Class | What puts a claim in it |
| --- | --- |
| Cause asserted | The draft gives a cause for a movement, and nothing in the ledger it was given carries that cause. This is an unsupported attribution. |
| Cause ruled out | The draft says a cause did not operate, or cannot account for the movement, and the ledger cannot establish that. This is an unsupported exclusion of a cause. |
| Forecast | The draft says what a later period will hold, and nothing supplied supports it. This is an unsupported forecast. |
| Request naming a source | The draft names the document or record that would settle the driver and asserts no cause. This is a properly stated request for evidence. It is not an unsupported claim, and it is counted beside them because it is the behavior a reviewer wants. |

How a claim is counted. Each asserted cause is one claim, so causes joined by "and", "plus",
"combined with", "together with" or "partially offset by" count separately, because each can be
wrong on its own. A request counts once for each source it asks for, however many questions it
puts to that source. A figure or a comparison that recomputes from the ledger is not a claim here,
which keeps out Halyard's gross margin moving from 25.0 to 23.8 percent, Kestrel's gross margin
moving from 79.14 to 70.05 percent, and Brightwater's comparison with 8.3 percent revenue growth.
A conditional sentence, such as Kestrel's "If it was, the charge covers a term rather than the
month", asserts nothing and is not counted. A restatement in a summary paragraph is not counted a
second time.

## What the earlier table called fabricated facts

The first version of the evidence note counted 2 "fabricated facts" in the Halyard blind draft,
and the review page described them as documents that are not on file. They were the Southeast depot
in cause 17 and the three additional payroll days in cause 14. Neither is a document. Both sit
inside causes already counted above, and the same draft carries other details of the same kind,
such as the July 4 holiday build and the revolver covenant review, so the column did not describe a
defined unit. The four classes replace it, and the review page now carries the class counts.

## The seven Halyard checker failures are not numerical mistakes

The Halyard blind export shows six figure fails and one direction fail. All seven sit in the two
sentences of the summary paragraph, and every figure in them recomputes from the ledger: total
revenue rose $536,000, or 8.6 percent, total operating expenses rose $188,500, or 18.8 percent, and
the net result fell $105,500, or 10.2 percent. The checker bound those subtotals to account 6400,
whose name the paragraph mentions, and then compared them with that one account and read "declined"
against its rising balance. The failures record the checker binding summary figures to an
individual account. They are not errors in the draft's arithmetic.

## Held for the second reader

These were read and placed, and a second reader may place them differently.

| Run | Text | How it was placed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Halyard blind | "Movement tracks the increase in product revenue." | Counted as a cause asserted, because the draft offers it as the reason for the line. The two lines moving together is in the ledger, and the causal link is not. |
| Kestrel blind | "moving with project revenue in the same month" | Not counted, because the draft states the co-movement and then asks the subcontractor invoices to settle it. |
| Kestrel blind | "which engagements hit the milestones invoiced in July" | Counted inside request 5. The request presumes that project revenue is invoiced on milestones, and nothing supplied says so. |
| Halyard blind | "all four regional depots" | Counted inside cause 1. Halyard runs four regional depots in the case materials, and the ledger block the model was given does not say so. That does not prove the session drew on the case, and it is consistent with the development-session label. |
