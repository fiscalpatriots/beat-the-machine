## 1. What the screen does now

Under the ledger on the orientation screen, headed "Your read, before any AI draft", one chip per
ledger account and no cap: "Tap every line you would give a second look, judging from these figures
alone. A line you leave untapped counts as one you would let stand. Nothing here is scored, and at
the end you will see where your calls moved once you had read the draft." The onward button reads
"Tap at least one line" until one is tapped, then "Lock in my read and continue". On a phone it is
the fixed bottom bar; on a desk it sits under the status line. Taps save as they happen. Focus starts
on the screen heading. Once the player goes on, the read is locked.

A tapped account reads as a flag on every round one line on that account (Halyard's lines 7 and 12
share 4000, Kestrel's 4 and 8 share 4100) and an untapped one as let it stand. Points, rank and
badges are untouched: none of the scoring functions changed.

## 3. The fact it makes possible, on the end screen

Under the round's counts, "Your read before the draft, and your final calls". From the Halyard run,
accounts 4100, 4200 and 7000 tapped and lines 1 and 4 turned over:

> Before the memo you tapped three accounts on the ledger for a second look. After reading the AI
> draft, your final call moved off that read on eight of the fourteen lines. Seven of those changes
> moved toward the answer key and one moved away from it. On the other six lines, your final call
> matched your first read.

The lines that moved sit behind one disclosure, "See the eight lines that moved", each reading, for
example, "Line 1, Freight billed to customers. Tapped before the draft, let stand after it. That
change moved away from the key." A note under it says a tapped account counts as a flag and that the
comparison adds no points. The walk computes the expected counts from the case file independently of
the page and checks the paragraph word for word, including the wording when every change runs one
way ("That change moved away from the answer key.").

## 7. The findings measure and its tests

`tools/findings.py` rebuilds the read from the accounts tapped against the recorded case version,
checks the letters and counts the page posted, and reports for first attempts only, inside each case
set: first attempts carrying the required read, how many moved at least one call off it, lines
compared, lines moved, toward the key, away from it, and held, with a table by line. Rows posted by
the optional step, reads locked after a call and rows with no read are counted apart and enter no
count. Every block opens with the label: descriptive agreement on a keyed exercise, not a pre-test
and post-test, never a learning gain, and a change toward the key is not credited to the draft alone
because the card's file and the practice reveals came before each final call. The readout fields
carry the prefix `prepick.` (13 of them), replacing `r1.prepicks_given`, and `READOUT-TEMPLATE.md`
section 8 names them.

Six new tests in `tools/tests/test_findings.py` (`PrepickTest`): both cell shapes parse; the read is
set against the final calls toward, away and held with per-line counts and fields; first attempts
only and each case version apart, including two cards on one account; the optional-era, late and
missing rows enter no count; posted letters and counts that disagree are reported while the case
file decides; the block says descriptive and never learning. **37 of 37 pass**, by
`python tools/tests/test_findings.py` and by `pytest`.
