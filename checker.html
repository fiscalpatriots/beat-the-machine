Second Pass Checker

Paste a ledger and the memo somebody drafted about it. The checker recomputes every line, ties each sentence to an account, and tests the arithmetic, the direction and the silence. What is left over is the part that needs a person, and it prints as a short list of questions.

Nothing you paste leaves this page. The checker is one HTML file with no libraries, no network calls and no storage. Close the tab and the ledger is gone.

Tab separated, comma separated or column aligned. Dollar signs, commas and (parentheses) for negatives are all read.

Free text. Lines that start with a label such as S1. are kept whole; anything else is split into sentences.

Account numbers only, with plus, minus, times, divide and parentheses. A percent in the memo that is not a line percent is tested against every ratio, for both months and for the change between them.

How to use it in a close

Run it first, before anybody reads the memo. The four mechanical checks settle what can be settled from the numbers, and the reviewer's time then goes only to the queue at the bottom: whether the driver is supported by something on file, and whether the period is right. Those two need a person.
