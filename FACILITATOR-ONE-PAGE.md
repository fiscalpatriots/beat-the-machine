# Second Pass: Beat the Machine, a Facilitator's Guide

Forty minutes for a chapter meeting, a classroom or a firm's staff training. Version 2.1, 13 September 2026, against `halyard-v4`, `brightwater-v5`, product 1.6.1. Long version: `FACILITATOR-GUIDE.md`.

## What you need, and what to do first

- The link, https://fiscalpatriots.github.io/beat-the-machine/, and a phone or laptop each. The header carries Drill, Checker and Review, so no URL is read aloud.
- The response sheet, if you want numbers. Rounds post to a Google Form you must own to read. Without it, take a show of hands. Walk it yourself first in test mode, `?test=1`, which posts nothing.

## The two-step lock, and the reason score

The player calls the line, the buttons become a chip row, and no reveal comes until a chip is tapped. Say that in the framing. The chips: figure does not tie, direction wrong, no source on file, wrong period, wrong account, nothing written where owed, and on a stand, the figure and reason hold. Each card carries a key of the chips that fit it, so a reason scores right when one tapped chip is in the key and none is outside it. The end screen prints the call score beside the reason score. The badges read the calls alone, an agreeing reason adds points toward the rank, and the reason score tests the stated basis for contradiction rather than proving anybody reasoned.

## The debrief, six questions in order

| Ask the room | Type and lines | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which figures did not tie? | Arithmetic, 1 and 8 | Check the number against the ledger before you read the reason attached to it. |
| Which sentence runs backwards? | Wrong direction, 3 | The memo said declined beside a June balance higher than May's. |
| Which two sentences cannot both be true? | Timing, 2 | The same program is finished on June 30 and still running into August, and neither says what June work was performed. |
| Which reasons sounded specific and rested on nothing? | Unsupported driver, 7 and 14 | Ask what document would prove it. On 14 it points at depreciation, which never moved. |
| Where is a movement credited to a cause nobody documented? | Unsupported attribution, 12 | Continued demand is a claim about customers, and no billing bridge is on file. |
| Which account owed an explanation and got none? | No explanation, 11 | Read the ledger for what the memo left out, not only for what it got wrong. |

Then three minutes on the reason. Ask which chip they tapped, type by type, and put the key beside it: 1 and 8 figure does not tie, 3 direction wrong, 2 wrong period, 11 nothing written where owed; no source on file rides with all of those except 1 and stands alone on 7, 12 and 14; clean lines take the figure and reason hold. Watch for a right call through the wrong finding, a defect chip on a line they let stand, and no source on file tapped everywhere.
