The ledger and the memo

0 characters

Tabs, commas or aligned columns, one account per line. Dollar signs, thousands commas and (parentheses) for negatives are all read, and a header row is ignored.

0 characters

Free text. A line starting S1., 1. or (a) is kept whole; anything else is split into sentences. One card comes back per sentence, and one more for every account that clears the rule with nothing written about it.

What the checks found

    Key each line

    The checks fill in a suggestion where they found something. A suggestion is not the key: confirm it or change it, and write the two lines in your own words.

    Add a fresh set, optional

    A second company the player has not read, run after the first set. Leave this closed and the drill goes from the last line straight to the end screen.
