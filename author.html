Build a drill from your own ledger

Paste a close ledger and the memo somebody drafted about it. The page reads both with the checker's own reader and gives every sentence one of the checker's four statuses. A sentence the checker would hold for review is held here too, with no call suggested. Then you key each line: the call, the error type, the basis, and the two lines a player reads when the answer comes back.

The ledger and the memo

0 characters

Tabs, commas or aligned columns, one account per line. Dollar signs, thousands commas and (parentheses) for negatives are all read, and a header row is ignored.

0 characters

Free text. A line starting S1., 1. or (a) is kept whole; anything else is split into sentences. Every sentence comes back: a card for each one that binds to a ledger line, and an open item for each one that binds to none, which you leave out with a reason or rewrite and read again. One more card comes back for every account that clears the rule with nothing written about it.

What the checks found

    Key each line

    Each card carries the status the checker gives its sentence. A card suggests a stand only where every check it ran was checked within scope, and a flag only where a check failed. A sentence held for review or not checked comes with no suggestion, and the call is yours. A suggestion is not the key either: confirm it or change it, and write the two lines in your own words.

    Add a fresh set, optional

    A second company the player has not read, run after the first set. Leave this closed and the drill goes from the last line straight to the end screen.

    Save it

    Saving writes the case into this browser under the key the drill reads for My own case, and downloads the same JSON so the case can be committed to cases/ or handed to somebody else. The file carries the ledger figures the cards were read from and a version stamp of the exact inputs and threshold that read took. Change any of them after Read it and saving stays off until the case is read again.

    Everything you paste is read by the script in your own browser, and the case you save is written to this browser's own storage on this device. This page is served from GitHub Pages and loads one Google font; nothing you paste is sent anywhere, and anything you copy into another AI service goes to that service under its terms.
